01 · Philosophy

Privacy First

I built Taven on the belief that your productivity data is sacred. The app is designed so that I never have access to your tasks, notes, name, or email address. Your data stays where it belongs: with you.

02 · Collection

What I Collect

Nothing. Taven has no account or sign-in, and no server of mine ever receives your tasks, notes, name, email address, or any other identifying information.

Taven also contains no third-party SDKs. That means no analytics, no advertising, no attribution, no crash-reporting services, and no tracking of any kind.

One thing to know: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may send me anonymized crash and usage reports. These never include your tasks, and I can't tie them to you. You can turn this off at any time.
No tracking, no third-party SDKs
03 · Infrastructure

Where Your Data Lives

Your tasks are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If you choose to turn on syncing, Taven uses iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your devices up to date. Synced data lives in the private database tied to your own Apple Account, which I have no way to view or access.

Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and at rest, and hosts it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

04 · Intelligence

On-Device AI

Taven's intelligence features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Your requests are processed by the model built into your device, so they work offline and entirely on your device.

Your tasks are never sent to me, to a third-party LLM, or to any external cloud service, and Taven does not use Private Cloud Compute or any other server-based AI. I never receive your tasks, so they can't be used to train or improve any AI model.

100% On-Device AI
05 · Permissions

Permissions & System Features

Taven asks iOS for as little as possible:

  • Notifications: used only to deliver the task reminders you set. They're scheduled locally on your device, Taven doesn't use a push notification server, and I never see them.
  • Widgets, Siri & Shortcuts: these read your tasks on your device so you can see or add them from your Home Screen, Siri, or the Shortcuts app. Siri and Shortcuts are Apple features, so Apple's privacy policy governs how Siri handles your voice requests.

Taven doesn't request access to your Calendar or Reminders.

06 · Retention

Retention & Deletion

I don't store your data, so there is no retention period on my end. Your data stays on your device until you delete it, and you're in control at every step:

  • In the app: edit or delete any task at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes Taven's data from that device.
  • If you used sync, a copy remains in your iCloud until you remove it. Go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Taven → Delete Data from iCloud. (Menu labels may vary slightly between iOS versions.)
07 · Rights

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the EU/UK and the CCPA/CPRA in California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

Because I don't collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing on my end to access or delete. You control your data directly through the app and your iCloud settings (see Retention & Deletion). I never sell or share personal information.

If you have a question or request, email tavenproductivity@gmail.com. If you're in the EU/UK, you can also contact your local data protection authority.
