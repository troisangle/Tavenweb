The best AI to-do list apps

Every task app seems to say “AI powered” now, and it means wildly different things. Some pick your next task. Some rearrange your calendar. Some break big jobs into small steps. Here's what each one actually does.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Start with the job you want the AI to do. If you want it to tell you which task to do next, privately on your iPhone and for free, use Taven. If you want it to schedule your tasks into your calendar, try Motion or Reclaim.ai. If big tasks paralyze you and you want them broken into small steps, use Goblin Tools. And if you want a full traditional task manager with some AI help sprinkled on top, Todoist is the safe choice.

Quick disclosure: we make Taven, so take our enthusiasm with a grain of salt. That said, every app on this page is good at something, and we've tried to say plainly where Taven isn't the right pick. Prices and features change a lot, so double check the App Store before you commit to anything.

What “AI” usually means in a task app

Once you look past the marketing, the AI in to-do apps tends to do one of four things.

The first is prioritization. The app looks at everything on your list and tells you which task matters most right now. This goes after decision fatigue, which is that miserable stretch of time you spend staring at a list trying to work out where to start.

The second is scheduling. The app finds free slots in your calendar, drops your tasks into them, and shuffles things around when your day changes.

The third is breaking tasks down. You type “clean the garage” and get back a list of actual, doable steps.

The fourth is the general assistant, where a chatbot style AI inside a normal task manager helps you write, rewrite or organize tasks.

It's also worth asking where the AI runs. Cloud AI sends your task text off to a server somewhere. On-device AI runs on your phone, so your tasks never leave it. If your to-do list includes anything personal (and whose doesn't?), that difference matters.

So which one should you pick?

If you open your list and just freeze, try Taven. It picks one task and tells you why, and we've written more about that feeling in how to stop feeling overwhelmed by your to-do list.

If your calendar is wall to wall meetings, Motion or Reclaim.ai will probably help you more. If your problem is that tasks feel too big to even start, Goblin Tools is great, and it's free. If you want a full featured app that works everywhere with some AI on top, Todoist is the dependable option.

And if you'd rather your tasks weren't sent to an AI server at all, Taven is the one that keeps everything on your iPhone or iPad.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an AI app that tells you what to do next?

Yes. Taven, for iPhone and iPad, is built around exactly this. Its on-device AI looks at importance, due dates and how often tasks repeat, then shows you your single most important task with a short reason like “Due today and your top priority.”

Which AI to-do list app is the most private?

Taven runs its AI entirely on your device, doesn't need an account and doesn't collect any data. Most other AI task apps, including Motion, Reclaim and Todoist's AI features, process your tasks in the cloud.

Is there a free AI task manager?

Taven is free with no subscription. Goblin Tools is free on the web. Reclaim.ai and Todoist both have free plans with some limits.

What's the difference between AI prioritizing and AI scheduling?

Prioritizing decides which task matters most right now, which is what Taven does. Scheduling decides when each task happens by putting it on your calendar, which is what Motion and Reclaim.ai do.
