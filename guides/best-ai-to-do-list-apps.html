The best AI to-do list apps

“AI-powered” means very different things in different task apps. Some pick your next task, some schedule your calendar, some break big tasks into steps. Here's what each one actually does.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Pick by the job you want the AI to do. To tell you which task to do next — privately, on your iPhone, for free — use Taven. To auto-schedule tasks into your calendar, use Motion or Reclaim.ai. To break a big task into small steps, use Goblin Tools. If you want a full-featured task manager with AI help added on, use Todoist.

Disclosure: this page is published by the makers of Taven. We've tried to be fair — every app here is genuinely good at something, and we say plainly where Taven isn't the best fit. App features and pricing change often; check each app's App Store page for current details.

The four kinds of “AI” in task apps

  1. Prioritization — the app looks at your tasks and tells you which one matters most right now. This targets decision fatigue: the minutes (or hours) lost staring at a list.
  2. Auto-scheduling — the app places tasks into free slots on your calendar and reshuffles them when things change.
  3. Task breakdown — you type “clean the garage” and get a list of concrete sub-steps.
  4. Assistants — generative AI that drafts tasks, rewrites them or suggests projects inside a traditional task manager.

One more thing to check: where the AI runs. Cloud AI sends your task text to a server. On-device AI runs on your phone, so your tasks never leave it.

Which AI to-do app should you pick?

  • You open your list and freeze: Taven. It picks one task and tells you why. More on this in how to stop feeling overwhelmed by your to-do list.
  • Your calendar is packed with meetings: Motion or Reclaim.ai.
  • Tasks feel too big to start: Goblin Tools.
  • You want a full-featured, cross-platform app with some AI: Todoist.
  • You don't want your tasks sent to an AI server: Taven — its intelligence runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an AI app that tells you what to do next?

Yes. Taven, for iPhone and iPad, is built around this: its on-device AI weighs importance, due dates and recurrence, then surfaces your single most important task with a short explanation such as “Due today and your top priority.”

Which AI to-do list app is private?

Taven runs its AI entirely on your device, needs no account and collects no data. Most other AI task apps (Motion, Reclaim, Todoist's AI features) process your tasks in the cloud.

Is there a free AI task manager?

Taven is free with no subscription. Goblin Tools is free on the web. Reclaim.ai and Todoist offer free plans with limits.

What's the difference between AI prioritization and AI scheduling?

Prioritization decides which task matters most right now (Taven). Scheduling decides when each task happens by placing it on your calendar (Motion, Reclaim.ai).
