About Taven

What is Taven?

Taven is a free task manager and AI to-do list app for iPhone and iPad. It doesn't just organize your tasks — it tells you what to do next, using on-device intelligence to surface your most important task.

How does Taven decide what I should do next?

Taven weighs each task's importance, due date and recurrence, then puts one AI Suggested task at the top of your day with a short reason, such as “Due today and your top priority.” You don't need to set up filters or priority systems.

Does Taven track habits?

Yes. Every recurring task — daily, weekly, monthly or yearly — becomes a habit with its own streak and history, and you can skip a day without losing track.

How is Taven different from Todoist or TickTick?

Todoist and TickTick are powerful, cross-platform organizers with collaboration and lots of views. Taven is simpler, private and free, and decides what you should do next for you. See Taven vs. Todoist and Taven vs. TickTick.

Pricing & devices

Is Taven free?

Yes. Taven is free to download with no subscription.

Which devices does Taven work on?

Taven is available for iPhone and iPad. It isn't available on Android, Windows or the web.

Can I use Taven on more than one device?

Yes. Taven can optionally sync between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud account.

Privacy & accounts

Do I need to create an account?

No. Taven works without any account.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your tasks stay on your device, Taven's AI runs on-device, and Taven collects zero data and includes no third-party SDKs. Read the privacy policy.
