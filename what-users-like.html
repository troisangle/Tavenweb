01

A simple, beautiful design

What users tell us

The thing people mention first is how Taven looks and feels. Users tell us the interface is clean, calm and genuinely pleasant to open — no clutter, no maze of menus.

Why it matters

A task app you enjoy opening is a task app you actually use. Taven's rounded, minimal design keeps attention on your tasks, and you can make it yours with your own accent color and a light, dark or system theme.

02

The Suggest feature

What users tell us

Users love opening Taven and being told what to work on. The AI Suggested task at the top of the day takes away the “where do I even start?” moment.

Why it matters

Taven's on-device AI weighs each task's importance, due date and recurrence, then picks one task and explains why — for example, “Due today and your top priority.” It runs privately on your iPhone or iPad.

03

Habits and one-time tasks, in harmony

What users tell us

Most apps make you keep habits in one place and to-dos in another. Users tell us they love that Taven puts both side by side, so the morning run and the project deadline live in the same day.

Why it matters

Every recurring task — daily, weekly, monthly or yearly — becomes a habit with its own streak and history, right alongside your one-time tasks. Skip a day without losing track, and add end dates when a habit has a finish line.

04

The calendar

What users tell us

Users like being able to see what's coming up on a calendar, with their tasks and habits laid out by date.

Why it matters

Seeing the shape of your week makes it easier to plan realistically — and Taven's calendar is included free, with no premium tier.

05

Completion statistics

What users tell us

Users enjoy watching their progress add up. The completion stats turn finished tasks into something you can see — and want to keep growing.

Why it matters

Completion rings, weekly charts, monthly heatmaps, your completion rate and your best day show when you do your best work. The “Today's Intensity” gauge tells you when you've planned more than you can finish.
