Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Taven doesn't just organize your tasks — it tells you what to do next. Here's everything it does to keep your day focused.

Intelligent Prioritization

Stop guessing what to do next. Taven weighs importance, due dates and recurrence to surface exactly what to tackle first.

Smart Recurrence

Daily habits, weekly check-ins, monthly bills, yearly milestones. Set it once and Taven keeps it on your calendar, right next to your one-time tasks.

Privacy First

Your data lives on your device. No accounts. No cloud data collection. Everything is processed locally.

Insightful Analytics

Completion rings, weekly charts and monthly heatmaps reveal when you do your best work.

iCloud Sync

Optionally sync across your Apple devices through your private iCloud account — accessible only to you.

Make It Yours

Choose your accent color and a light, dark or system theme. Every screen adapts to match.
