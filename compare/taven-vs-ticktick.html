Taven vs. TickTick

TickTick gives you more ways to look at your tasks. Taven gives you one answer: what to do next. Here's how they compare, and why people who feel buried in features switch to Taven.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Taven is the better choice if you're on iPhone or iPad and want a calm app that tells you what to do next instead of adding more views to manage. It's free with no Premium tier, needs no account, builds habit streaks into every recurring task, and runs its AI privately on your device. TickTick is the better fit if you need a built-in Pomodoro timer, or Android, Windows or web access.

Disclosure: Taven is our app, so we're biased — but we've kept every fact on this page accurate and noted where TickTick is the better fit. Features and pricing change often; check the App Store for current details.

Why choose Taven over TickTick

1. One answer instead of more views

TickTick offers lists, a calendar, an Eisenhower matrix, a habit tracker and a focus timer — powerful, but every view is another place to look and another decision to make. Taven's on-device AI weighs importance, due dates and recurrence and puts one AI Suggested task at the top, with the reason why. Less looking, more doing.

2. Free, with no Premium paywall

Taven is free with no subscription. Many of TickTick's standout features, including its full calendar views, require a Premium subscription. Taven's calendar is included free.

3. Private by design

Taven has no accounts, no servers holding your tasks and no third-party SDKs, and it collects zero data. Its AI runs on your device. TickTick requires an account and syncs your tasks through its cloud.

4. Habits that live inside your tasks

TickTick keeps habits in a separate tracker you maintain alongside your task list. In Taven, a recurring task is the habit: it gets its own streak and history automatically, and you can skip a day without losing track.

5. A calmer app

Fewer features is a feature. Taven has less to configure, fewer places for tasks to hide and nothing pulling your attention away from the one thing you should be doing.

6. Analytics that keep you honest

Completion rings, weekly charts, monthly heatmaps, your best day and a “Today's Intensity” gauge that shows when you've planned more than you can finish.

A typical morning in each app

TickTick

  1. Open the app
  2. Choose a view: list, calendar or matrix
  3. Check your habits in the habit tracker
  4. Sort out what's urgent versus important
  5. Pick a task and start a focus timer

Taven

  1. Open the app
  2. See your AI Suggested task and why it's first
  3. Start it — your habits are already in your task list

When TickTick might be the better fit

TickTick is a capable all-in-one app. It may suit you better if:

  • You want a Pomodoro focus timer inside your task app.
  • You need Android, Windows or web access — Taven is iPhone and iPad only.
  • You share lists with other people.

If what you actually want is to stop deliberating and start working, Taven's focus on one clear next step is the advantage.

Switching from TickTick to Taven

  1. Download Taven — it's free and there's no account to create.
  2. Add what's due in the next week first. You don't need to move your whole backlog on day one.
  3. Turn each TickTick habit into a recurring task in Taven — it gets a streak automatically.
  4. Turn on iCloud sync if you use both iPhone and iPad.
  5. Tomorrow morning, open Taven and do the task it suggests.

The verdict

Choose Taven if…

  • You're on iPhone or iPad
  • Too many views and features make you procrastinate
  • You want to be told what to do next
  • You want privacy and no subscription
  • You want habits tracked right inside your tasks

Stick with TickTick if…

  • You rely on a built-in Pomodoro timer
  • You need Android, Windows or web access
  • You share lists with others

Frequently asked questions

Is Taven a good TickTick alternative?

Yes — especially if you're on iPhone or iPad and feel TickTick has more features than you use. Taven is free with no Premium tier, needs no account, keeps your data on your device, builds habit streaks into recurring tasks, and tells you what to do next.

Why switch from TickTick to Taven?

People switch for a simpler app that makes the prioritization decision for them, for privacy (no account, on-device AI, zero data collected), and to avoid paying for Premium.

Does Taven have a habit tracker?

Yes. Every recurring task in Taven (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly) becomes a habit with its own streak and history, and you can skip a day without losing track.

Does Taven have a Pomodoro timer?

No. Taven focuses on choosing the right task to work on. If a built-in focus timer is essential to you, TickTick includes one.
