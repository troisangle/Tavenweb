Taven vs. TickTick

TickTick packs tasks, a calendar, a habit tracker and a focus timer into one app. Taven does fewer things and makes the main decision for you. Here's how they compare.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Choose TickTick if you want an all-in-one app with a Pomodoro timer, calendar views and an Eisenhower matrix, or you need Android, Windows or web access. Choose Taven if you're on iPhone or iPad and want the app to tell you what to do next rather than give you more views to manage — privately, with no account, for free.

Disclosure: this page is published by the makers of Taven. We've tried to be fair — both apps are genuinely good at something, and we say plainly where Taven isn't the best fit. App features and pricing change often; check each app's App Store page for current details.

Where TickTick is better

  • More tools in one place. The Pomodoro timer, calendar views and dedicated habit tracker mean you may not need any other productivity app.
  • Cross-platform. TickTick runs on Android, Windows, Mac and the web. Taven is iPhone and iPad only.
  • Manual prioritization views. If you like sorting tasks yourself, TickTick's Eisenhower matrix view is excellent.
  • Natural-language entry and shared lists. Quick to capture dated tasks, and you can share lists with others.

Where Taven is better

  • It makes the decision. TickTick gives you many ways to look at your tasks. Taven gives you one answer: the task to do next, with the reason.
  • Calmer and simpler. Fewer features means less to configure and fewer places for tasks to hide.
  • Privacy. No account, no servers holding your tasks, no third-party SDKs, on-device AI. TickTick requires an account and syncs through its cloud.
  • Price. Taven is free with no subscription. Many of TickTick's standout features require Premium.
  • Habits connected to tasks. In Taven, a recurring task is the habit, with its streak and history, rather than a separate tracker to maintain.

Which should you choose?

Choose TickTick if…

  • You want a Pomodoro timer and calendar built into your task app
  • You use Android, Windows or the web
  • You like organizing tasks with views like the Eisenhower matrix
  • You share lists with others

Choose Taven if…

  • You're on iPhone or iPad
  • Too many features and views make you procrastinate
  • You want the app to tell you what to do next
  • You want privacy and no subscription

Frequently asked questions

Is Taven a good TickTick alternative?

Yes, if you're on iPhone or iPad and want something simpler that decides what to do next for you, privately and for free. If you depend on TickTick's Pomodoro timer, calendar views or cross-platform sync, stay with TickTick.

Does Taven have a habit tracker?

Yes. Every recurring task in Taven (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly) becomes a habit with its own streak and history, and you can skip a day without losing track.

Does Taven have a Pomodoro timer?

No. Taven focuses on choosing the right task. If a built-in focus timer is important to you, TickTick includes one.

Is Taven free?

Yes. Taven is free with no subscription and no account.
