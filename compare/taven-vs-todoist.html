Taven vs. Todoist

Todoist helps you organize everything. Taven helps you actually get the right thing done — by telling you what to do next. Here's how they compare, and why people on iPhone and iPad are switching.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Taven is the better choice if you're on iPhone or iPad and want an app that decides what you should do next instead of making you build a priority system. It's free with no subscription, needs no account, and its AI runs privately on your device. Todoist is the better fit only if you need Android, Windows or web access, or share projects with a team.

Disclosure: Taven is our app, so we're biased — but we've kept every fact on this page accurate and noted where Todoist is the better fit. Features and pricing change often; check the App Store for current details.

Why choose Taven over Todoist

1. It tells you what to do next — automatically

Todoist gives you priority flags (P1–P4), labels and filter queries, and then leaves the decision to you. Taven does the deciding: its on-device AI weighs each task's importance, due date and recurrence, then puts one AI Suggested task at the top of your day with a plain-English reason, like “Due today and your top priority.” You open the app and start, instead of scanning a list.

2. Free — really free

Taven costs nothing, with no subscription and no feature paywall. Todoist's free plan is limited, and its full feature set — including its AI features — requires a paid plan billed every year.

3. Your tasks stay yours

Taven has no accounts, no servers holding your tasks and no third-party SDKs, and it collects zero data. Its AI runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Todoist requires an account and stores your tasks in its cloud, where its AI features also run.

4. Habits built into your tasks

Every recurring task in Taven — daily, weekly, monthly or yearly — automatically becomes a habit with its own streak and history. Skip a day without losing track. Todoist offers Karma points and daily goals, but not per-task streaks.

5. Analytics that show when you do your best work

Completion rings, weekly charts, monthly heatmaps, your completion rate, your best day and a “Today's Intensity” gauge that warns you when you've planned too much.

6. Nothing to set up or maintain

Todoist rewards people who build and maintain a system. Taven is designed so you don't have to: add tasks, mark what matters, and let it handle the ordering. Less time organizing means more time doing.

When Todoist might be the better fit

To be fair, Todoist is a mature, well-made app. Stick with it if:

  • You need your tasks on Android, Windows or the web — Taven is iPhone and iPad only.
  • You share projects with a team and need assignees and comments.
  • You depend on integrations with tools like Slack, email or your calendar.

If you mostly use Todoist for your own tasks on an iPhone or iPad, none of those apply — and Taven will likely save you both time and money. See what Taven users love most.

Switching from Todoist to Taven

  1. Download Taven — it's free and there's no account to create.
  2. Add what's due in the next week first. You don't need to move your whole backlog on day one.
  3. Recreate your recurring tasks; each one becomes a habit with a streak automatically.
  4. Turn on iCloud sync if you use both iPhone and iPad.
  5. Tomorrow morning, open Taven and do the task it suggests.

The verdict

Choose Taven if…

  • You're on iPhone or iPad
  • You want to be told what to do next, not build a system
  • You want your tasks private, with no account
  • You'd rather not pay a subscription
  • You want habits and streaks built into your tasks

Stick with Todoist if…

  • You need Android, Windows or web access
  • You manage shared projects with a team
  • You rely on third-party integrations

Frequently asked questions

Is Taven a good Todoist alternative?

Yes — especially if you're on iPhone or iPad and use Todoist for personal tasks. Taven is free with no subscription, needs no account, keeps your data on your device, and automatically suggests what to do next. If you need Android, Windows or team collaboration, Todoist remains the better fit.

Why switch from Todoist to Taven?

The main reasons are automatic prioritization (Taven picks your next task and explains why), privacy (no account, on-device AI, zero data collected), price (free, no subscription) and built-in habit streaks for every recurring task.

Is Taven free?

Yes. Taven is free to download with no subscription and no account.

Does Taven work on Android or Windows?

No. Taven is available for iPhone and iPad, and syncs between them through your private iCloud account.
