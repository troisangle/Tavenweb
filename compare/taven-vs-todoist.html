Taven vs. Todoist

Todoist is brilliant at helping you organize everything. Taven is about helping you get the right thing done, by telling you what to do next. Here's how they stack up, and why a lot of iPhone and iPad users are making the switch.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

If you're on iPhone or iPad and you'd like an app that decides what you should do next instead of asking you to build a priority system, Taven is the better pick. It's free with no subscription, there's no account, and the AI runs privately on your device. Todoist only really pulls ahead if you need Android, Windows or the web, or you share projects with a team.

Quick disclosure: Taven is our app, so yes, we're biased. We've still kept every fact on this page accurate, and we've pointed out where Todoist is the better choice. Features and prices change often, so check the App Store for the latest.

Why you might choose Taven over Todoist

1. It tells you what to do next

Todoist gives you priority flags (P1 to P4), labels and filter queries, and then it's up to you to decide. Taven makes the call for you. Its on-device AI looks at how important each task is, when it's due and whether it repeats, then puts one AI Suggested task at the top of your day with a reason in plain English, like “Due today and your top priority.” You open the app and get going, rather than scrolling through a list.

2. It's actually free

Taven costs nothing. There's no subscription and nothing hidden behind a paywall. Todoist's free plan is fairly limited, and you'll need a paid plan for the full feature set, AI features included.

3. Your tasks stay yours

Taven has no accounts, no servers storing your tasks and no third-party SDKs, and it collects no data at all. The AI runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Todoist needs an account and keeps your tasks in its cloud, which is also where its AI runs.

4. Habits are built right into your tasks

Every recurring task in Taven, whether it's daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, automatically becomes a habit with its own streak and history. You can skip a day without losing track. Todoist has Karma points and daily goals, but not a streak for each task.

5. Stats that show when you do your best work

You get completion rings, weekly charts, monthly heatmaps, your completion rate, your best day, and a “Today's Intensity” gauge that warns you when you've piled on too much.

6. There's nothing to set up

Todoist really rewards people who enjoy building and maintaining a system. Taven is designed for people who don't. Add your tasks, mark what matters, and let it sort out the order. Less time organizing, more time actually doing things.

A typical morning in each app

Todoist

  1. Open the Today view
  2. Look through everything due today and overdue
  3. Check your priority flags and labels
  4. Weigh the deadlines against each other
  5. Pick something, which is often the easiest thing

  2. See your AI Suggested task and why it's first
  3. Start it

When Todoist might suit you better

In fairness, Todoist is a mature, really well made app, and there are good reasons to stay with it. If you need your tasks on Android, Windows or the web, Todoist has you covered and Taven doesn't (it's iPhone and iPad only). If you share projects with a team and need assignees and comments, Todoist is built for that. And if your workflow depends on integrations with Slack, email or your calendar, Todoist has a lot more of them.

But if you mostly use Todoist for your own tasks on an iPhone or iPad, none of that really applies, and Taven will probably save you both time and money. You can also see what Taven users love most.

Moving from Todoist to Taven

  1. Download Taven. It's free and there's no account to set up.
  2. Add whatever's due in the next week first. There's no need to move your whole backlog on day one.
  3. Set up your recurring tasks again. Each one turns into a habit with a streak on its own.
  4. Switch on iCloud sync if you use both an iPhone and an iPad.
  5. Tomorrow morning, open Taven and do whatever it suggests.

The verdict

Go with Taven if…

  • You're on iPhone or iPad
  • You'd rather be told what to do next than build a system
  • You want your tasks kept private, with no account
  • You'd rather not pay a subscription
  • You like having habits and streaks built into your tasks

Stick with Todoist if…

  • You need Android, Windows or web access
  • You run shared projects with a team
  • You rely on third-party integrations

Frequently asked questions

Is Taven a good alternative to Todoist?

Yes, especially if you're on iPhone or iPad and mostly use Todoist for personal stuff. Taven is free with no subscription, doesn't need an account, keeps your data on your device and suggests what to do next automatically. If you need Android, Windows or team features, Todoist is still the better fit.

Why do people switch from Todoist to Taven?

Mostly for four reasons. Taven picks your next task and explains why. It's private, with no account, on-device AI and no data collected. It's free with no subscription. And every recurring task gets its own habit streak.

Is Taven free?

Yes. It's free to download, with no subscription and no account.

Does Taven work on Android or Windows?

No. Taven is available for iPhone and iPad, and it syncs between the two through your private iCloud account.
