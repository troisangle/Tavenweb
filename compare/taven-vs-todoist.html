Taven vs. Todoist

Todoist is one of the best task managers ever made. Taven is built for a narrower job. Here's an honest look at where each one wins.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Short answer

Choose Todoist if you need your tasks on Android, Windows or the web, share projects with other people, or rely on integrations and advanced filters. Choose Taven if you're on iPhone or iPad and want an app that tells you what to do next, keeps your data on your device with no account, and is free with no subscription.

Disclosure: this page is published by the makers of Taven. We've tried to be fair — both apps are genuinely good at something, and we say plainly where Taven isn't the best fit. App features and pricing change often; check each app's App Store page for current details.

Where Todoist is better

  • Everywhere you work. Todoist runs on almost every platform, including Android, Windows and the web. Taven is iPhone and iPad only.
  • Working with other people. Shared projects, assignees and comments make Todoist a real team tool. Taven is personal.
  • Natural-language quick add. Typing “Submit expenses every last Friday #Work p1” and having it parsed perfectly is one of Todoist's best features.
  • Power-user organization. Labels, sections, custom filter queries and board views let you build almost any system.
  • Integrations and maturity. Todoist has a large integration ecosystem, an API and many years of polish.

Where Taven is better

  • Deciding what to do next. In Todoist, you set priorities and build filters, then decide yourself. Taven's AI weighs importance, due dates and recurrence and shows one suggested task with a reason. No setup.
  • Privacy. Taven has no accounts, no servers holding your tasks, no third-party SDKs, and its AI runs on-device. Todoist requires an account and stores tasks in the cloud.
  • Price. Taven is free with no subscription. Todoist's free plan is limited, and features like reminders and AI help come with paid plans.
  • Habits built in. Every recurring task in Taven becomes a habit with its own streak and history, and you can skip a day without losing track.
  • Simplicity. Taven has fewer features on purpose. There's less to configure and less to maintain.

Which should you choose?

Choose Todoist if…

  • You use Android, Windows or a browser, not just Apple devices
  • You share tasks or projects with a team, partner or family
  • You want to build a detailed system with labels and filters
  • You need integrations with other tools

Choose Taven if…

  • You're on iPhone or iPad
  • You often freeze at your list and want to be told what to do next
  • You want your tasks private, with no account
  • You want a capable app for free, with no subscription

Many people try both: Todoist as a team or work hub, Taven for personal tasks and habits.

Frequently asked questions

Is Taven a good Todoist alternative?

Yes, if you're on iPhone or iPad and use Todoist mainly for personal tasks. Taven is free, needs no account and automatically suggests what to do next. If you rely on Todoist's cross-platform sync, shared projects or integrations, Taven isn't a replacement.

Is Taven free?

Yes. Taven is free to download with no subscription and no account.

Does Taven work on Android or Windows?

No. Taven is currently available for iPhone and iPad. If you need Android, Windows or web access, Todoist is the better choice.

Can I share tasks with others in Taven?

Not currently. Taven is designed for personal task management. For shared projects, Todoist is a strong option.
