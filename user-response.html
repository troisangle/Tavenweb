What people love about Taven.

We read every message. These are the five things users bring up most.

Simple, beautiful design

The first thing people mention. Clean, calm and genuinely nice to open — no clutter, no maze of menus.

The Suggest feature

Open the app and Taven tells you what to work on, with the reason why. No more staring at a long list.

Habits and tasks together

Your morning run and your project deadline live in the same day. Every recurring task keeps its own streak.

The calendar

See what's coming up, with tasks and habits laid out by date. Included free.

Completion stats

Rings, weekly charts and heatmaps turn finished tasks into progress you can see — and want to keep growing.
